Shipping & returns

Shipping

Orders within Switzerland and Liechtenstein ship with Swiss Post. Orders to other countries ship with DHL Express. Shipping costs and delivery times are shown in the cart before you pay.

Deliveries outside Switzerland may be subject to import duties and VAT in the destination country. These are paid by the recipient.

Returns

Define your return policy, e.g. returns within 14 days for unused devices in original packaging, customer pays return shipping.

Warranty

Define your warranty, e.g. 2 years on manufacturing defects.
